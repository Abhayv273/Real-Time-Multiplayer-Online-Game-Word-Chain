How to play

  1. Chain: type a real English word (3+ letters) starting with the last letter of the previous word.
  2. Time: 10 seconds per turn. Time out = lose 1 life.
  3. Lives: everyone starts with 3.
  4. Points: 1 to 3 letters = 1, 4 letters = 2, 5+ letters = 4 and +1 life (max 3).
  5. No repeats: a word can be used only once per match.
  6. Pause: 2 per player. Only the player who paused can resume.
  7. Win: match ends when one player is left with lives. Highest score wins, equal scores = draw.
  8. Quit: if everyone else quits, you win.
